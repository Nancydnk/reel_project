# Multimodal Fine Dining Consumer Insight Observer

## Problem Statement
Fine dining operators in Bangkok face high customer acquisition costs and lack actionable qualitative insights. Traditional reviews and star ratings fail to capture real-time emotional reactions during the dining customer journey.

## Target Audience
Fine dining restaurant owners, marketing directors, and culinary executives in Bangkok seeking deeper consumer sentiment analysis.

## Proposed Solution
An AI-driven Multimodal Insight Observer agent. The system combines video review metadata, real-time facial emotion evaluation via webcam, and qualitative chat interviews to synthesize single-page executive decision reports.

## Expected Outcomes
- Real-time sentiment breakdown across Food Quality, Service, Ambience, and Value.
- Strategic marketing recommendations for restaurant management.
